Policy Acknowledgement Portal – AI Summary Card

Project Overview

The Policy Acknowledgement Portal is an AI-powered web application that helps users understand company policies using natural language interaction. The system provides clear policy explanations, summaries, and secure AI responses.

---

Main AI Endpoints

1. Policy Explanation Endpoint

Purpose:
Explains company policies in simple language.

Example:
"Explain leave policy"

---

2. Policy Summary Endpoint

Purpose:
Generates short summaries of long policies.

Example:
"Summarize work from home policy"

---

3. Security Validation Endpoint

Purpose:
Detects invalid, malicious, or unsafe inputs.

Example:
SQL Injection and Prompt Injection protection
